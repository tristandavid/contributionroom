Questions

Does it connect to my bank or brokerage?

No. You enter your contributions yourself, so nothing about your accounts leaves your phone.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. You can export a backup file and restore it on another phone. If you turn on sync, an encrypted copy is also kept in a file you choose in your own cloud storage (such as Google Drive, iCloud Drive or Dropbox). It is locked with your passphrase, which never leaves your phone, so neither we nor the storage provider can read it. Receipt photos always stay on the phone.

How accurate is the refund estimate?

It uses the published federal and provincial (or US federal) tax brackets for the year and the income you enter. Credits, surtaxes and benefits can change your actual refund, so treat it as a guide, not tax advice.

Does scanning my notice send it anywhere?

No. The text is read on your phone, and the photo is not uploaded or saved. Check the figures it finds before you use them.

Is the room it shows exact?

For accounts where the government publishes your personal room (such as TFSA, RRSP and FHSA in Canada), enter that figure once and the app carries it forward. Otherwise it shows an estimate from the annual limit and your entries. It is a tracking tool, not tax advice, so confirm with the CRA or the IRS.

How do new limits get added?

The app reads a small public file of limits, tax brackets and deadlines from this site. When the government publishes new figures, that file is updated and the app uses them without a new release.

How do I cancel Premium?

In Google Play or your Apple ID subscription settings. The app has a Manage subscription button.
