Questions

Does it connect to my bank or brokerage?

No. You enter your contributions yourself, so nothing about your accounts leaves your phone.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. You can export a backup file and restore it on another phone.

Is the room it shows exact?

For accounts where the government publishes your personal room (such as TFSA, RRSP and FHSA in Canada), enter that figure once and the app carries it forward. Otherwise it shows an estimate from the annual limit and your entries. It is a tracking tool, not tax advice, so confirm with the CRA or the IRS.

How do new limits get added?

The app reads a small public file of limits and deadlines from this site. When a limit changes, that file is updated and the app uses it without a new release.

How do I cancel Premium?

In Google Play or your Apple ID subscription settings. The app has a Manage subscription button.
